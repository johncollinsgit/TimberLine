How Everbranch took shape.

Everbranch’s development under the Everbranch name dates back to at least April 2026.

Everbranch is a connected business platform created through Evergrove Software. This account separates software development and brand adoption from public launch and actual customer use.

What these dates establish

The April milestone supports a history of development under the Everbranch name. It is not an exact founding date, a public-launch date or evidence of first commercial use. March 2026 customer use has not been verified.

Some repository notes have March headings but received Everbranch naming edits in April. Those headings should not be read as proof that the Everbranch name was in use in March.

Milestones still being established

The exact founding date, first public deployment under this name and first actual customer use remain unverified in this review. Planning documents, integration setup, commits and domain records are different kinds of evidence and do not establish commercial use on their own.

This page will be updated when additional dated records support a more precise account. Historical source records retain their original dates and contents.
