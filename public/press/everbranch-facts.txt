EVERBRANCH | PRESS FACTS
Published and reviewed: November 14, 2026

Everbranch is a connected business platform created through Evergrove Software.
It brings shared business tools, specialized apps and custom development together.
Capabilities vary by workspace and release; some branches are demos, pilots or
in development.

APPROVED HISTORY WORDING
Everbranch’s development under the Everbranch name dates back to at least April 2026.

That is a development and brand-history statement. It does not establish an exact
founding date, public launch date, first customer use or first commercial use.
March 2026 customer use remains unverified. Do not derive any of those dates from
domain registration, commits, integration setup or planning documents alone.
