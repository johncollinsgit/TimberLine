# Everbranch story voiceover

## 0:00–0:08 — Problem

After eleven years in small business, our founder wanted a calmer way to run the work. Because every day creates more signals: an order, a customer question, a campaign click, a follow-up that should not be forgotten.

## 0:08–0:22 — Connect

Everbranch brings those moments together. Your store, website, customer activity, marketing context, and real work all connect into one view.

## 0:22–0:42 — Customer story

Instead of piecing together a relationship from different tools, you see one customer story. What they did, what they bought, what they engaged with, and what your team needs to know next.

## 0:42–1:00 — Action

That context becomes useful action. Notice a customer who has not come back. Build the right audience. Prepare a thoughtful campaign or follow-up. Your team stays in control of every message.

## 1:00–1:17 — Intelligence

Then learn from the outcome. Everbranch connects performance with the people and moments behind it, so you can see what is working and decide what to do next.

## 1:17–1:30 — System and end card

Storefront, customers, work, marketing, and learning—working as one system. One customer story. Better decisions. Everbranch. Grow with the whole story.
