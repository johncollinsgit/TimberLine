Modern Forestry Pay catalog shell

Derived from the owner's Everbranch Pay style demo saved October 24, 2026.
Retains its forest artwork, public product photographs, variant selection and
three approved bundle offers. Product facts are a snapshot, not live inventory.

Fictional events, staff switching, sales history, simulated payments, fake
receipts and sample tax rates were removed. Basket review shows a subtotal and
requires verified tax/stock/prices at a connected register. Its ordinary product
basket passes only variant IDs and integer quantities to native checkout. The
server reviews the live catalog and freezes the selected customer with the sale.
Bundle offers remain browseable but cannot enter live checkout until their
provider pricing and component-stock mapping are reviewed.

The native messages are setup and a strictly validated checkout basket. Their
handler accepts the bundled main-frame file only and opens native setup,
recovery or checkout. External navigation and network API requests from the
page are blocked. Credentials, device authorizations, recovery records and
Square payment UI remain native. Catalog browsing does not establish collection
readiness; native checkout honors the server's independent collection gates.

Images are cached copies of the original public catalog photos for browsing
without a network connection. The app icon is the owner's existing Modern
Forestry icon, copied without modification. New device builds and UI checks do
not establish merchant authorization, deployment, stock review or payment
acceptance. Follow the existing POS runbooks before collecting money.
