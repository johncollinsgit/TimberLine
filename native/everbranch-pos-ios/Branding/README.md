# Everbranch Pay icon

Owner direction, October 31, 2026: use an Everbranch-style leaf containing a
dollar sign for the payment app. The merchant's Modern Forestry branding remains
inside the register. The prior home-screen icon used the merchant's black tree.

The asset catalog's `AppIcon-1024.png` is an opaque, full-square 1024 px PNG.
iOS applies the home-screen mask. Do not bake rounded corners into this asset.
The Everbranch Field icon at `public/field/brand/field-512.png` was the visual
reference; that application's icon is unchanged.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then mechanically resized for
the iOS asset catalog. Generation prompt:

> Create a production iPhone app icon for Everbranch Pay, using the attached
> Everbranch Field icon as the brand/style reference. Asset: a flat, vector-clean,
> full-bleed square icon, 1024 by 1024, fully opaque with no baked-in rounded
> corners. Keep Everbranch's rich evergreen background and warm ivory leaf. The
> single leaf should have the same gentle upward-right silhouette and short
> lower-left stem as the reference, large and optically centered, occupying
> roughly 66% of the square height. Simplify/remove the central diagonal vein so
> there is an uninterrupted ivory leaf interior. Place one clearly legible
> evergreen dollar sign '$' centered INSIDE the leaf, integrated as negative
> space; its size must read well at home-screen icon size. The dollar sign stays
> upright with two short vertical extensions and a clean, substantial sans-serif
> S. Very restrained evergreen tonal background is fine, but no texture, shine,
> bevel, shadows, extra badges, outlines, border, text, wordmark, money
> illustrations or additional leaves. Remove the faint horizontal line below
> the leaf from the reference. This should look like a close member of the
> Everbranch app family, distinguished by the '$' within the leaf.

This asset update does not establish payment acceptance or Apple distribution
approval. Each installed candidate retains a unique build number and the normal
signing, onboarding and commercial collection gates.
