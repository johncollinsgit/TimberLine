# In-person reward redemption foundation

This work is not enabled for customer collection. The Modern Forestry rewards API reports `pos_authorization_available: false`, and its issuance endpoint returns unavailable. There is no POS quote, payment outbox, or customer-link creation integration yet. Do not remove the rollout gate until the conditions below are met and verified.

An authenticated customer may eventually present a short-lived opaque QR proof. The server stores only its hash, enforces tenant/customer/runtime scope, and binds the proof to one original sale. The current QR is issued before a sale or amount is known: it is a one-use bearer capability, not evidence that a customer approved a particular transaction. Before enabling, either add explicit authenticated confirmation bound to the sale and amount, or review and clearly disclose capped one-use scan authorization and test theft and replay.

A quote must use an explicitly verified account-to-profile link; existing email-only, demo and unverified matches do not qualify. Historical provider mappings may have originated from email or phone matching and their provenance can be overwritten. A trusted link-creation and review flow with independent account-ownership evidence, conflicting external-profile checks and an audit trail remains to be implemented. The tenant policy must separately enable and review a POS maximum, refund rule and underlying published rewards version; each register must review that version. Modern Forestry's requested limit is $5 per sale, not a change to its online redemption increment.

The standalone reservation service locks the same customer balance used by online Candle Cash and records an idempotent hold against the original sale. Payment evidence can finalize a hold; definite cancellation or failure can release it; uncertain payments remain held. A verified full refund can restore the reward once. An amount-only partial refund requires review because the returned items and tax allocation are not proven. These methods are not yet wired to payment, refund, cancellation or recovery effects. A Square refund must never be blocked by a reward review.

Customer merge is another activation blocker: the existing merge flow can move transactions and delete a donor balance while a hold still points to that donor. Reservation, release, refund and merge must coordinate using the same ordered profile and wallet locks. Define whether active holds block a merge or are migrated with an audit trail, revalidate canonical identity while locked, and test merges before, during and after payment. Until then, a hold must never be assumed safely restorable after a merge.

## Required durable start/cancel fence

The current POS start/discard paths use a 180-second cache lease. A lease expiring during a stalled collection is not evidence that no payment can start. Do not release reward holds solely because the lease expired, a screen closed, a callback is missing, or a provider lookup returned no match.

The disabled `PosPaymentStartClaim` foundation serializes a database-backed `starting` or `cancelled` claim under the tenant-scoped original sale row. Starting claims do not expire and repeated starts refer to the original payment request. A cancelled claim cannot become a new start. This foundation is **not wired** and is not sufficient on its own: before using it to release value, every production start, cancel, expiry, recovery, native descriptor issuance and relevant stock path must enforce the same durable claim. Old or concurrently deployed code must not be able to start without it. Journal reservation and provider calls must preserve the payment core's separate committed boundary, remain bound to the same original request, and be safely recoverable if a process stops between claim, journal reservation and descriptor issuance. A failed or interrupted claimed start stays held until evidence establishes a safe terminal outcome.

The future quote must calculate the reward after eligible discounts and before tax, freeze the customer, eligible subtotal, reward amount and reviewed policy, and atomically commit the sale, proof binding, shared-wallet debit and reward hold. Failed quote creation must roll all of those changes back. It must not store a raw QR bearer token in snapshots, logs, receipts or durable device state. Existing customer, inventory, offer and payment isolation checks still apply.

Activation requires concurrency tests across multiple server processes, retry and interruption tests around every boundary, fresh provider payment/refund evidence, and a physical consented iPhone rehearsal. The existing standalone tests use fictional data and do not establish live payment readiness.
