# Birthday redemption across channels

Birthday discounts at the register remain disabled. Default-off foundations include a separate birthday proof, annual online-operation fence, online activation and use observation hooks, and a single-attempt deactivation adapter. `PosBirthdayChannelEvidence` classifies provider observations but cannot authorize redemption. An inactive Shopify code with no reported uses does not prove that an earlier checkout will not arrive later.

The existing `birthday_cycle_coverage` unique key guards annual issuance. A future POS-only benefit must share that annual cycle and cannot duplicate an existing or uncertain online issuance. The additive `pos_birthday_annual_holds` table reserves space for durable POS evidence, but there is **no production hold writer**. Under the annual coverage lock, the issuance guard rejects any such evidence even if the POS feature is later switched off; released and unrecognized states remain review-only. When the default-off channel fence is enabled, online activation also rejects POS evidence, and observed late online use enters review. These checks are protective foundations, not proof of channel exclusivity: no POS-only customer proof, atomic quote/hold binding, or Square-confirmed hold lifecycle is wired.

Online activation records an in-flight operation before Shopify work outside its transaction; uncertain provider outcomes remain in review. Reconciliation observes online use after the fact, so it cannot prevent a checkout already accepted by Shopify. An inactive code and zero reported usage are insufficient to rule one out. Existing Shopify-issued codes therefore remain review-only for POS.

Required implementation and verification:

1. Implement a POS-only hold writer that proves the cycle has no existing, legacy, uncertain or published online reward, and coordinates every issuer and activation path on the same identity and annual lock. Protect against concurrent profile linking and merges. Keep historical Shopify-issued codes review-only. A bare coverage row with no issuance is not itself a hold.
2. Create and wire separate, short-lived customer-present authorization for the POS-only benefit to a freshly verified authenticated customer account. The current proof has no customer endpoint and requires an existing discount-code issuance, so it cannot authorize the POS-only path. Its exact customer-visible disclosure must cover the benefit, verified currency and value, tenant, cycle and one-use terms. The wallet authorization is limited to a capped wallet spend and is not permission to redeem a birthday benefit. Store only a token hash; bind it once with the original quote and durable annual hold in a single transaction.
3. Complete controlled use of the default-off Shopify adapter: it deactivates the exact store and discount node with a single attempt and no network call in a database transaction, then reads fresh provider state. It retains the operation in review even on success. Validate exact tenant, store, node, cycle and issuance; do not identify a benefit by name, email or card.
4. Ensure online order ingestion and delayed/reordered webhooks use the same fence. Existing online uses, unknown provider status, in-flight checkout, conflicting or late online uses, and paid POS after release require review. A fresh inactive state is not proof of exclusivity because an online checkout may already be in flight. Test real Shopify semantics before any rollout claim.
5. Bind the hold to the original Square payment and only consume on fresh confirmed payment. Release only on a verified terminal unpaid result or a verified unstarted discard; uncertain outcomes remain held. Refunds require an explicit restoration policy and must not silently issue a second annual reward.
6. Cover parallel online activation and POS attempts, provider timeouts, process crashes, identity merges, duplicate/reordered webhooks, delayed order ingestion, cancellation and refunds, cross-tenant access, expired/used proofs, and repeated retries with disposable databases and fakes. Then perform a controlled end-to-end rehearsal before any release gate is enabled.
