# Everbranch history and publication standard

Reviewed November 14, 2026.

> Everbranch’s development under the Everbranch name dates back to at least April 2026.

The authoritative public account is [Everbranch history](https://theeverbranch.com/everbranch-history.html).
The [press facts](https://theeverbranch.com/press/everbranch-facts.txt) reuse that wording.

## Public evidence

- [April 15, 2026 naming and canonical-domain change](https://github.com/johncollinsgit/TimberLine/commit/045ba9d88e73624fa1cc64eb03b75f6a1196e6b5): changes Grovebud runtime configuration and documentation to Everbranch. This corroborates development under the name, not deployment or customer use.
- [May 22, 2026 public splash source](https://github.com/johncollinsgit/TimberLine/commit/e9b967090c5db2bcd94e3ac1e45f69c61bdaa1e0): establishes public-facing development, not the date of activation.
- The April change also replaced domains beneath retained March headings in README and other documentation. Those headings are not evidence of March Everbranch naming or commercial use. Preserve the historical records and use this current clarification when interpreting them.

## Publication rules

- Keep one detailed public history page, with contextual links from the marketing sites and current README. Avoid repeating the sentence throughout unrelated pages.
- Publish with the actual publication date. Do not rewrite earlier commit dates, source records, messages or archived material.
- Do not publish private correspondence, customer records or personal information as evidence. Retain any private source references in the owner's private audit record only.
- Do not infer commercial use from commits, domain records, planning or integration setup. Do not make trademark-priority claims or change filings or legal declarations.
- App and company profiles may use the approved sentence and public history link. A development-related role date, if shown, must be explicitly distinguished from founding and commercial use.
