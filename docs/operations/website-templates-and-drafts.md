# Website templates and saved drafts

New self-service Website setup recommends one of seven neutral business starters from the current tenant blueprint. Each has six private pages, local navigation and relevant owned example photographs. The maker, electrical and studio starters reuse unbranded owned assets; landscaping, professional-practice and apparel starters use original generic photographs. Example gallery text identifies examples rather than claiming the photographs are a customer's actual work. Actual product images are supplied by the business; an industry photograph is not a fabricated product or stock record.

`config/website_starters.php` and `WebsiteStarterCatalog` are the shared photo/starter source. Existing branded demonstrations remain in `WebsiteThemeCatalog`; new unrelated customers are not offered their business identities, contact details, shipping terms or returns promises. Existing customer's selected style and saved content are not rewritten. The current design appears when reopening details.

Setup seeds a chosen design only when creating the first site. Reopening Edit details saves versioned business name, email, optional phone, hours and service area without reapplying a theme or changing any page draft, uploaded photo, navigation or published pointer. An existing site requires its actual current site-version ID; stale detail forms receive 409. Explicit Design replacement has a separate required confirmation and remains private until publishing. Trial capacity checks still prevent a replacement from creating more than six pages.

Customer name/contact details live in `TenantSiteVersion.settings.business_contact`; preview and public output use their respective draft/published snapshots. Header/footer use the saved business name, and contact information appears on the contact page. Published contact details never come from a later mutable setup row. Registered paid publication checks actual saved name/email in addition to the existing home/design/address requirements; old restored drafts with missing details must be completed before publication. Existing nonregistered pilot/legacy publish contracts retain their existing checks. Public shop/product renderer and routing choices also use published settings; a later private design change cannot switch the live storefront.

The reusable photo picker supports related starter photographs, the site's existing uploads, a new upload, replacement, removal and descriptions. Hero, image sections, cards/gallery items, logo, social image, product and collection controls share it. Product galleries store one editable cover description; individual page images and collection covers retain their own descriptions. All private uploads retain the native tenant/site ownership and storage limits. Page/SEO/logo slots use `/website-media/{id}`; catalog slots receive the exact server-issued `/website-catalog-media/{id}` alias. New logo/social image references cannot adopt another site's upload. Public admission requires the exact current published site/page reference, or active catalog product/collection reference on that site's permitted published host. A historical or unused upload stays private; no shared workspace-brand disk is opened. No image URL needs to be typed.

## History and retention

Native Website History shows recent page/settings autosaves and up to 30 recent published versions, plus current pointers. The editor saves pending edits before opening history. Page rows on the Website overview also expose History, including for expired owners who can preview but cannot restore. Metadata and actions are private and tenant scoped. Connected website adapters use their existing adapter recovery; native history GET and POST cannot reach their renderer/content.

The Carolina connected editor's image controls select its own saved/baseline approved photographs and keep its complete adapter content and revision/save path. They do not create native uploads, native history or a new public media audience. Native Website uploads/history remain separate from that adapter and from Shopify.

All authenticated Website tools require an active user and active membership in the actual tenant with an owner, tenant_owner, admin, manager or marketing_manager role. Public storefront/media lanes keep their independent host and ownership policies. Publishing retains its stricter owner/admin rule. History also checks the current actor in its controller and restore service. Global admin role alone, a viewer pivot, an inactive pivot or another tenant's membership grants no editor or history access. Each restore creates a new immutable **private draft**; it never changes any published pointer, activates paid access, moves a trial clock or calls a provider. Paid publication remains an explicit separate action.

Page and site restore POSTs require `current_version_id`. The relevant current pointer, original owned source and new snapshot are checked/written in one site-row transaction for every admitted cohort, including legacy accounts. Native page/settings autosaves use the same transaction and expected-pointer check, so another tab's delayed save cannot silently overwrite a restored state. A 409 keeps local edits for copying/review and requires a fresh deliberate reload; the client never adopts a new expected version from an error response.

Only the original audited private-trial cohort receives bounded autosave retention on future ordinary draft writes: 30 recent unpublished page drafts per page and 10 recent unpublished site-settings drafts. The policy requires the exact successful original trial-start audit and original tenant/profile/owner/clock contents, excluding the later conversion timestamp. Metadata alone, an unrelated copied clock or a registered historical tenant does not qualify.

All published-status rows, current draft/published pointers and versions referenced by actual site publication/page rollback events are retained. Audit rows remain even when their obsolete unpublished autosave payload is pruned; routine draft-saved events do not pin every autosave forever. Pruning does not delete photos, audit records, pages, published content or customer records. No job, backfill or historical legacy pruning is introduced. Protected referenced drafts can exceed the ordinary 30/10 windows. History's bounded recent list is distinct from retained published storage.

At trial expiry, mutations and restores close at the existing server-clock boundary; retained owned history and previews remain readable. Enrollment still creates no automatic charge. Payment is required to publish/connect domains and use gated paid services. A trial conversion keeps the original clock and Website data. These features never enable merchant checkout, domains, provider spending or public rendering by themselves.
